# Mosaic 0.4.1

## Meetings

- Schedule meetings in the Meetings module (title, date, start and end time, type, location, attendees, agenda). Scheduled meetings appear in their own list until they are recorded.
- In Notetaker, pick a scheduled meeting to fill in the meeting details; saving the recording turns that scheduled meeting into its archive instead of creating a new one.

## Appearance

- Support for local colour themes: `.css` theme files in the Mosaic profile's `themes` folder appear in Settings → Appearance. They stay on the PC and are never part of the repository.
- Placeholder for importing and exporting your own theme (coming later).

## Fixes

- Deleting the last meeting now clears the open meeting record.
- The "Nothing was shared" recording notice has a close button.
- Dark text in IT Projects (timeline phases and deliverables) is readable again in dark mode.
